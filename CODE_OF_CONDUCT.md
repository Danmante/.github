# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and professional community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- Be mindful of safety, privacy, and patient impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive conduct.
- Threats, intimidation, or public shaming.
- Disruptive behavior that undermines the project.

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the maintainers and may result in moderation, restriction, or removal from the community.
