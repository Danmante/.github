# Governance

This repository follows a lightweight governance model designed for safety, clarity, and maintainability.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- reviewing pull requests
- overseeing repository standards
- approving high-risk changes
- ensuring compliance-sensitive updates follow the required review process

## Decision making

- Routine changes may be approved through the normal pull-request workflow.
- Safety-critical, jurisdiction-sensitive, or compliance-sensitive changes require explicit review.
- Major product or policy changes should be documented in the project decision log.
